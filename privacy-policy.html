4. Firebase Analytics

CG Gyan uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App and to improve its functionality.

Analytics may collect information such as app launches, screens viewed, quiz interactions, general feature usage, session information, device information, app information, and other events configured by the developer.

CG Gyan does not intentionally use Firebase Analytics to collect a user's name, phone number, password, or other directly identifying information.

5. Firebase Crashlytics

CG Gyan uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify and diagnose crashes and technical problems.

Crashlytics may process information such as:

  • Device information
  • Operating-system information
  • App version
  • Crash reports
  • Diagnostic information
  • App state at the time of a crash

This information helps us identify bugs, improve stability, and provide a better user experience.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or use information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, and advertising interaction information.

Advertising providers may use this information to provide, measure, and improve advertisements in accordance with their own policies and applicable laws.

7. Rewarded Advertisements

CG Gyan may allow users to voluntarily watch a rewarded advertisement in exchange for an in-app benefit.

Watching a rewarded advertisement is optional. The advertisement itself may be provided and processed by Google AdMob and its partners according to their applicable policies.

8. Supabase

CG Gyan uses Supabase to store and retrieve quiz-related content.

The information stored in Supabase may include:

  • Quiz questions
  • Answer options

  • Subjects

CG Gyan does not require users to create an account to access this content.
